What I'm working on

Flagship review · In progress

Antibody–drug conjugate resistance in breast cancer

How do HER2+ breast tumours stop responding to antibody–drug conjugates such as T-DM1 and trastuzumab deruxtecan? I treat it as a network problem: build the protein–protein interaction network around the resistance genes, run GO and pathway enrichment, and read off the processes that keep recurring — antigen handling and internalisation, payload response, and the signalling that routes around the drug.

Approach

  • PPI network construction
  • GO & pathway enrichment
  • Hub & module analysis

UBE3A & Angelman syndrome — a bioinformatics case study

An end-to-end analysis of the UBE3A gene behind Angelman syndrome: genomic structure and ORF, the HECT-domain protein, imprinting and UBE3A-ATS silencing, pathogenic variants from ClinVar and dbSNP, the interaction network, and the antisense-oligonucleotide and gene-therapy strategies now in trials.

NCBI · UniProt · STRING · ClinVar · KEGG · 2025

Analysis · Solo

Alcohol dehydrogenase across yeast & plants

A comparative sequence study of alcohol dehydrogenase (ADH): multiple-sequence alignment across yeast and plant species to trace the conserved regions, conserved-domain annotation to locate the functional core, and PCR primer design against the aligned sequences.

Clustal Omega · NCBI CD-Search · primer design
